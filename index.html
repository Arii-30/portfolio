about

A developer who ships, not just studies

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Duhok, where I spent four years building real, working software instead of just coursework — POS systems, a library manager, a chess engine, and a couple of web platforms, mostly alongside two or three classmates at a time.

I run NuVision together with a small team: we build websites and business tools for real clients and post tech content on the side. My focus is the backend and database layer, but I work the full stack when a project needs it.

I pick up new tools fast, lean on AI-assisted workflows where they genuinely help, and care more about code that holds up in production than code that just runs once in a demo.
